Jarvis

A private voice assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows computer.

Jarvis is a personal, single-user project. It is not a public service, it has no sign-ups and it is not offered to anyone else. It runs locally at localhost on one computer and answers its owner by voice, on screen and through a private Telegram bot.

What Jarvis does with Google

When the owner connects their own Google account, Jarvis can:

  • read the owner's Google Calendar and add or move events when asked;
  • read and search the owner's Gmail to answer questions like "any unread email?";
  • create drafts of replies in Gmail. Jarvis never sends email: the owner reviews and sends drafts manually.

Jarvis cannot delete email or calendar events.
